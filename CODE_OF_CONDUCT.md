# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences; assume good faith.
- Give and accept specific, technical feedback about code, not about people.
- Keep discussions on topic. Bug reports are about reproducible behaviour, not about
  the content somebody chose to download.
- Respect privacy: do not publish another person's URLs, tokens, cookies, or logs
  without permission, and redact secrets before pasting output.
- Accept that maintainers may decline features that conflict with the project's
  documented scope (no DRM/login/captcha circumvention, no RPC service, no bundled
  extras).

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Insulting, demeaning, or discriminatory comments; personal or political attacks.
- Harassment in public or private channels, including unwanted contact.
- Publishing someone's private information without explicit permission.
- Repeatedly derailing issues or reviews after a maintainer has asked to stop.
- Using the project to distribute malware, or asking maintainers to help do so.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions, and any
other space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainer via a private channel — a GitHub Security Advisory
or a direct message to [@qulm1](https://github.com/qulm1). Reports are reviewed
confidentially. Responses may range from a private warning, to editing or removing
content, to a temporary or permanent ban from the project spaces. Maintainers who do
not follow this code in good faith may face the same consequences.
